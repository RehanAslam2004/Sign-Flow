Recognition State Machine & Deduplication

  • Interim Preview: Displays tentative recognized sign without polluting the transcript.
  • Stability Window (500–800ms): Requires model predictions to stay stable before entering candidate status.
  • Boundary & Rest Reset: Recognizer commits exactly once, then disarms until hands move to rest or transition to a distinct subsequent gesture.
  • Utterance Pauses: Accumulates isolated words into a phrase; speaks on pause (~1–1.5s) or via manual "Finish & Speak" action.

Dignified Recovery & Error States

Camera Access Needed

Displays clear empty state with "Try again" button and browser settings guidance. No raw technical errors.

Camera Disconnected

"Your camera stopped working." Offers reconnect/switch options; transcript is preserved.

No Hands in Frame

Subtle overlay: "Position your hands in the camera frame." Never clears transcript or raises disruptive alarms.

Unclear / Unsupported Sign

"Sign not recognized — try signing again." Never hallucinates words or makes guesses via an LLM.

Long Inactivity

"Ready when you are." Calm idle state that keeps history visible and ready to resume immediately.

Speech Failure

Audio error notice; translated text remains prominently displayed on screen.
