App Map & Architecture Tour

SignFlow Architecture Map

Interactive code map and data flow overview for the SignFlow real-time assistive translation proof of concept.

🎥 Video & Landmarks

  • components/CameraViewport.tsx — Video stream and WebGL canvas overlay.
  • lib/mediapipe.ts — MediaPipe Tasks Vision WebAssembly loader and 21-landmark extractor.
  • components/GhostHandGuide.tsx — AR ghost-hand demonstration guide.

🧠 Recognition & Intelligence

  • lib/clientClassifier.ts — Zero-latency in-browser geometric edge classifier.
  • lib/grammarEngine.ts — Conversational sentence rules (English & Urdu).
  • lib/stateMachine.ts — Time-decayed consensus, majority voting & stability.
  • cv-service/main.py — Python FastAPI WebSocket backend service.

🗣️ Output & Voice

  • lib/speech.ts — Web Speech API SpeechSynthesis wrapper with session protection.
  • components/TranscriptView.tsx — Dual-layer conversation transcript with audio replay.
  • lib/bilingualFormatter.ts — Formatter for dual-language display & phonetic speech.

End-to-End Recognition & Speech Loop

1

Camera Capture & Hand Landmarks

Browser captures webcam feed. MediaPipe HandLandmarker extracts 21 3D coordinate landmarks per hand in local memory.

2

Dual-Track Recognition Engine

Local geometric heuristics evaluate gestures in real time (<5ms). Landmark packets stream over WebSocket to FastAPI for multi-class classification.

3

Temporal Consensus & Grammar Assembly

State machine stabilizes candidates across 250–380ms windows, resolving synonym clusters and composing natural conversational sentences.

4

Transcript Commit & Speech Output

Boundary pause or Finish & Speak commits the utterance to the transcript, triggers browser SpeechSynthesis, and presents contextual suggested replies.
